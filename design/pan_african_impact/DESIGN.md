## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a forward-looking, high-trust humanitarian aesthetic designed to engage institutional donors, pan-African partners, and grassroots communities. Bridging local impact in West Africa with international diplomacy, the visual identity rejects crisis-driven tropes in favor of dignity, agency, and institutional excellence.

The design movement mixes **warm minimalism** with **tactile elevation**:
- **Generous Whitespace & Warm Grounds:** Clean, breathable viewports anchored by organic warm off-white tones instead of clinical blue-whites.
- **Dignified Humanist Modernism:** High-legibility geometric typefaces paired with full-bleed, vibrant editorial photography showcasing real communities, field workers, and sustainable progress.
- **Polished Micro-surfaces:** Crisp rounded geometry, elevated floating cards, micro-borders, and pill-shaped interactive triggers that communicate approachability alongside rigorous governance.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Layouts naturally accommodate French typography standards (longer sentence strings, non-breaking punctuation spacing) and English without structural breakage or unbalanced line wraps.

## Colors
The color architecture balances deep institutional authority with optimism and global alignment.

- **Primary (`#0F5132`):** Deep Forest Green reflects regeneration, terrestrial longevity, and sovereign stability. Used for critical navigational structures, primary text emphasis, solid badges, and anchored headers.
- **Secondary / CTA (`#F2A900`):** Warm Golden Orange drives focus toward high-conversion humanitarian actions (Donations, Program Applications, Strategic Reports). Paired with `#D99500` for active/hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#286FFA`):** Electric Global Blue represents international diplomacy, multilateral partnerships, and digital data indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF8F3`):** Warm off-white acts as the main canvas layer, reducing eye strain and softening visual contrast compared to raw white.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level floating containers and cards sit cleanly on top of the warm neutral ground with a delicate `#EBE6DD` border.
- **Text Layers:** `#1E1E1E` ensures WCAG AAA contrast for body and headers; `#5A5A5A` provides balanced weight for secondary metadata, subtitles, and captions.
- **UN SDG Tokens:** Dedicated semantic tokens for official UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG / ODD) allow dynamic badge rendering, progress meters, and thematic program categorizations without muddying core brand cues.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is deployed across headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its geometric clarity combined with open counters and soft terminals ensures high warmth and legibility across all screen sizes.

- **Bilingual French & English Adaptability:** Body text is calibrated to `16px/26px` to offer generous leading, ensuring that French prose (which occupies roughly 15-20% more horizontal space than English) reads effortlessly without visual crowding.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** 
  - `display-hero` is reserved for signature homepage headlines and impactful fundraising calls.
  - Headings (`headline-xl` through `headline-sm`) utilize tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for a modern editorial feel.
  - Subheadings and labels use uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.05em`) strictly for tags, SDG badges, and categorical indicators.
  - Numbers and metrics within impact reports use tabular figures with bold weights (`700` or `800`) to highlight accountability.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container widths capped at `1280px` for optimal readability and editorial layout control.

- **Grid Rhythms:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) margin.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margin.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.25rem` (20px) margin.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Sections adhere to open breathing room using `space-xl` multiplied for large landing page segments (e.g., `5rem` to `7rem` between narrative chapters), reinforcing an uncrowded, premium institutional atmosphere.
- **Reflow Considerations:** Split bilingual blocks, donation steps, and multi-tier SDG grids switch to clean single-column stacked modules below `768px`.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation conveys physical presence without visual clutter. Instead of heavy dropshadows, the system employs **ambient diffuse shadows** mixed with **subtle warm borders**.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main `#FAF8F3` background canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Containers):** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` continuous border of `#EBE6DD` combined with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`. The faint tint of deep forest green in the ambient shadow ties cards to the natural palette.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Flyouts):** Used when users engage with program cards or donor impact stories: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`. Surfaces rise vertically by `-2px` on hover via a `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` transition.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Bilingual Popovers, Floating Navigation):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Sticky navigation bars incorporate a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over an 85% opacity `#FAF8F3` ground.

## Shapes
The shape language uses pill forms and generous curvatures to communicate human warmth, agility, and modern pan-African optimism.

- **Primary Radius (`roundedness: 3`):** 
  - Buttons, badges, segmented bilingual toggles, and status tags are strictly **pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)**.
  - Program cards, data stat displays, and hero imagery wrappers employ **`rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px)**, delivering an inviting, friendly edge.
  - Large modal containers and media feature wrappers scale to **`rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px)**.
- **Form Controls:** Text inputs, dropdowns, and text areas adopt `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to provide a grounded, functional structure distinct from pure-pill action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F2A900` fill, `#1E1E1E` text, `font-weight: 600`. Hover state transitions to `#D99500` with subtle elevation. Active state slightly scales (`0.98`).
- **Institutional Primary:** Pill-shaped, `#0F5132` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to a 90% opacity tone with an ambient green shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#0F5132` and text in `#0F5132`. Hover fills with `rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.06)`.
- **Sizes:** Large (`padding: 16px 32px; font-size: 16px`), Medium (`padding: 12px 24px; font-size: 15px`), Small (`padding: 8px 16px; font-size: 13px`).

### Cards
- **Program & Story Cards:** Base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EBE6DD`, radius `rounded-2xl`. Feature full-bleed photography at the top with an aspect ratio of `16:10`, transitioning into card padding of `space-lg`.
- **Card Hover:** Subtle translate `-2px` on Y-axis with Level 2 shadow expansion.

### SDG Badges & Chips
- **SDG Indicators:** Compact pill badges containing the UN SDG number and name (e.g., `ODD 3: Bonne Santé`). Background set to a 12% tint of the respective SDG color with text matching the full saturation SDG token.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `#FAF8F3` background and `#5A5A5A` text. Selected state switches to `#0F5132` fill and `#FFFFFF` text.

### Language Switcher (FR / EN)
- **Segmented Pill Toggle:** Floating pill container with `#EBE6DD` subtle border and `#FFFFFF` background. The active language is represented by a solid `#0F5132` pill with `#FFFFFF` text, transitioning with smooth sliding feedback.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Clean white backgrounds, `1.5px` borders in `#EBE6DD`, radius `12px`, padding `14px 16px`. Focus state triggers a solid `#0F5132` border with `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` dimensions. Primary fill `#0F5132` upon checked state with crisp white vector check icons.

### Impact Metrics & Donation Tiers
- **Metric Highlights:** Large bold numbers (`Plus Jakarta Sans 700`, `32px` to `48px`) in `#0F5132` paired with small uppercase labels in `#5A5A5A`.
- **Donation Selector:** Radio pill matrix featuring preset amounts (e.g., `15 000 FCFA`, `50 000 FCFA`, `100 000 FCFA` / `$25`, `$50`, `$100`). Active tier highlights with `#F2A900` fill and dark charcoal text.
